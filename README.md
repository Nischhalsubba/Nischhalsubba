<sub>PROFILE / PRODUCT DESIGN</sub>

## I design for the moment complexity becomes a decision.

My work sits between **product strategy, UX systems, interaction design, and implementation**. I turn dense workflows, unfamiliar concepts, permissions, data, and operational constraints into products people can understand and trust.

I care about more than the screen. I care about how the product explains itself, how the system behaves, and how the experience stays coherent as it grows.

> **The goal is not to hide complexity. It is to give complexity a clear shape.**
